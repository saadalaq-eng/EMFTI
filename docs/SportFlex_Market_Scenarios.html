%

Share whose parents pay for out-of-school coaching (participation rate P). Study range 5–12%.

SAR

Published Riyadh prices of SAR 300–800 a month over about nine months. Study range 2,700–6,000.

%

Share of coaching that happens at providers able to contract with a platform. Study range 60–80%.

%

Commission charged to the provider on value settled through the wallet. Submission 3 base 10%.

%

Optional second cut paid by the parent. Set to 0 to model commission only.

SOM build (year 3)

SOM is built from the supply side: the children actually routed through contracted providers, and how much of their annual spend settles through the wallet.

%

Share of serviceable children not yet settled on a sport or coach. Study range 35–65%.

Base: 60 Riyadh, 30 Jeddah, 12 Dammam–Khobar.

Base averages 3,750 children over 102 providers. Lower planning case: 20.

%
